ChronoTrend

Forecast your own numbers on your own device.

What it does

Paste a column of numbers — daily prescriptions, weekly units sold, monthly revenue — and ChronoTrend tests seven statistical forecasting methods against your own history, ranks them by how well they would have done, and forecasts with the winner. ChronoTrend Pro, a one-time purchase, adds two AI forecasting models, TimesFM 2.5 and Chronos-2, which are ranked the same way as everything else. Chronos-2 may not run on older hardware, such as iPads with an A12 chip.

Nothing leaves your device.
